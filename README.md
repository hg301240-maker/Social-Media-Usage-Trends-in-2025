# 📱 Social Media Usage Trends in 2025

The project explores major social media platforms, user demographics, short-form video growth, business impact, social commerce, AI integration, and digital marketing recommendations using statistics, charts, and visual storytelling.

## 📈 Key Insights

* Approximately **5.24 billion** people were identified as social media users worldwide in 2025.
* Around **241 million new users** joined social media during the previous year.
* Average daily social media usage is presented as approximately **2 hours 21 minutes**.
* Facebook is presented as the largest platform with approximately **3.07 billion users**.
* YouTube is presented with approximately **2.53 billion users**.
* Instagram and WhatsApp are presented with approximately **2.0 billion users each**.
* TikTok, WeChat, and Telegram are also highlighted as major platforms.
